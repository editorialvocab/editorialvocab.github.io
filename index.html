Exam Preparation Guides

UPSC

How to Use Editorial Vocabulary for UPSC

The Hindu editorial is the single most recommended resource for UPSC preparation. Here is the most effective way to use it daily:

  • Read today's Word of the Day — note its usage in context, not just the definition
  • UPSC prelims tests synonyms and antonyms heavily — study the Synonyms section for every word
  • Any word tagged UPSC PYQ is extremely likely to reappear — prioritise these
  • Build a target of 10 new words per day and review 5 old ones using the spaced repetition feature
  • For Mains GS-1 and Essay, strong vocabulary improves your answer quality significantly
BCS

BCS English Vocabulary Strategy

The English section of BCS (Bangladesh Civil Service) Preliminary consistently includes vocabulary questions sourced from The Daily Star editorials:

  • BCS 38–41 English sections each had 5–8 vocabulary MCQs from editorial words
  • Focus on words tagged BCS PYQ — these are verbatim questions from past exams
  • Learn the Bengali meaning alongside the English definition for faster recall under pressure
  • Complete the daily quiz every day — Q1 is always a real past BCS question
  • Track your 7-day streak in the app — consistency beats cramming for BCS English
SSC

SSC CGL English Vocabulary Tips

SSC CGL Tier-1 tests fill-in-the-blank, synonyms, and antonyms from a predictable high-frequency word list. Editorial vocabulary covers most of it:

  • SSC repeats words from 5–8 years ago — any PYQ-tagged word is worth extra attention
  • Practice one-word substitution: if the app shows multiple synonyms, pick the most formal one for SSC
  • Spend 10 minutes on the daily quiz — the MCQ format directly mirrors the SSC paper
  • Target 80% quiz accuracy consistently before your exam date
  • Download the app for XP streaks — gamification helps maintain daily practice
All Exams

5 Habits That Actually Improve Vocabulary

These habits consistently separate high scorers from average scorers in competitive exam English sections:

  • Context over rote: Read the full example sentence — you'll remember the word 3× longer
  • Audio reinforcement: Tap "Pronunciation" on every Word of the Day — hearing it embeds it differently
  • Teach it: Share today's word with one person (WhatsApp, group) — explaining = learning
  • Morning habit: Check the WOTD before checking social media — 5 minutes daily beats 2-hour cram sessions
  • Consistent streaks: A 14-day streak in the app earns a shield. Protecting your streak is the best motivation system
