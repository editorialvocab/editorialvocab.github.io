Extempore

/ɛkˈstɛmpəɹi/
adjective/adverb
Bengali Meaning
পূর্বপ্রস্তুতিহীন / তাৎক্ষণিক

Spoken or done without any prior preparation or practice.

Example
“He won the competition with an incredible extempore speech.”
Synonyms
improvisedimpromptuunpreparedspontaneousoffhand
Antonyms
preparedrehearsedpremeditatedplanned
