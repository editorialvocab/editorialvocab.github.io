Panegyric

/ˌpænəˈd͡ʒaɪɹɪk/
noun
Bengali Meaning
স্তুতিগাথা / উচ্চ প্রশংসা

A formal speech or opus publicly praising someone or something.

Example
“The book is a brilliant panegyric on the life and achievements of the freedom fighter.”
Synonyms
eulogyaccoladetributepaeanencomium
Antonyms
tiradecondemnationcriticismdenunciation
